# SEC-013 — Least privilege support

## Requirement
Minimum necessary access by default, everywhere: users, admins and services. No human or service account may hold permissions it has not used in the last 90 days without a documented justification.

## How MoDocument meets it today
- **Users.**
  - Read, export and modify are separate permissions. `READ_ONLY` holds 9 of 41: no export, upload, AI use or write (`src/main/java/com/modocument/application/security/RolePermissions.java`, `docs/compliance/mo-018-rbac-role-permission-matrix.md` §3).
  - Within a bank, even an ADMIN cannot open another user's private template, job or document. The live probe got 404 (`docs/compliance/evidence/A4-rbac-probe.md`, scenarios 7–8).
  - The workbook statement "exports limited to Admin + Read-write; only Admin manages users" is confirmed by the matrix. User administration is also held by `PLATFORM_ADMIN`, for the platform bank only.
- **Default role.**
  - An administrator chooses each local user's role, and the JML runbook says to start at `READ_ONLY` (`docs/security/jml-runbook.md`).
  - OIDC users get the provider's configured `default-role` (`docs/security/identity-providers.md`).
  - The product has no hard-coded default.
- **Platform staff.** `PLATFORM_ADMIN` holds no domain permission, so it reaches no bank data (probe scenarios 10–11, 34).
- **Services.**
  - The application connects to PostgreSQL as `modocument_app`, which has DML only, is `NOBYPASSRLS` and owns nothing (`src/main/resources/db/migration/V12__app_role_grants.sql`). This is the DB grants listing.
  - CI deploys through a short-lived GitHub OIDC role whose policy is limited to ECR push, ECS deploy in one cluster and passing named task roles (`infra/terraform/modules/ci-oidc/main.tf`).
  - Machine API clients get only their granted scopes and an expiry date (`src/main/java/com/modocument/api/security/PermissionChecker.java`; `API_CLIENT_CREATE` in `docs/security/maker-checker.md`). This is the one time-bound grant the product supports.
- **Recertification tooling.**
  - The access-review worksheet (`GET /api/admin/users/access-review`) lists every account with role, MFA, last login and an `inactive90d` flag. Generating it is audited (`ACCESS_REVIEW_GENERATED`).
  - The quarterly procedure is in `docs/security/jml-runbook.md`.
  - The probe generated the worksheet: 7 accounts, all fields present.
- **Not in place.**
  - Time-bound elevation of human roles: a role is held until someone changes it.
  - A completed quarterly recertification: the product is pre-production.
  - IAM policy linting in CI: no workflow in `.github/workflows` runs an IAM or Terraform policy linter.
  - The workbook's shared gap with SEC-017: admins use their everyday accounts. `TO CONFIRM (organisational)`

## Questionnaire answer (final)
Least privilege is built into MoDocument's role model:

- Read, export and modify are separate permissions, and read-only users cannot export, upload or change anything.
- Inside a bank, users see only their own jobs, documents and private templates.
- MO Intelligence's platform role has no access to any bank's data.
- The application connects to its database as a restricted account that can only read and write data and is bound by row-level security.
- CI deploys with short-lived OIDC credentials and holds no long-lived cloud keys.
- Machine integrations receive only their granted scopes, with an expiry date.

Administrators can produce a recertification worksheet that flags any account unused for 90 days. Time-bound elevation of administrator rights and the first quarterly recertification are still open actions; the proposed date for both is 30 November 2026. Evidence SEC-013-E01.

## Test SEC-013-T01
- **Procedure:**
  1. Identify the population: the 6 roles, the DB application role and the CI role.
  2. Check that each holds only what its function needs.
  3. Re-perform the access review on the live stack.
  4. Look for a record of a 90-day recertification.
- **Expected result:** no account, human or service, holds permissions unused in the last 90 days without documented justification.
- **Actual result:**
  - The role, DB and CI grants are minimal as described above.
  - The worksheet flags dormant accounts (`inactive90d`, covered by `UserAdminIntegrationTest.accessReviewFlagsNinetyDayInactiveAccounts`).
  - No production population exists yet, and no recertification has been recorded, so the 90-day criterion cannot be re-performed.
  - There is no time-bound elevation for human roles.
- **Result:** Partial · tester: Agent A4 (automated re-performance); human sign-off required · date: 2026-10-19

## Gap and remediation
- **Gap:**
  - No time-bound elevation of human admin rights.
  - No quarterly recertification on record.
  - No IAM policy lint in CI.
  - Admin rights are held on everyday accounts (organisational; shared with SEC-017).
- **Gap type:** Build.
- **Action:**
  - Build time-limited role grants: a role change with a `validUntil` that reverts automatically, under maker/checker.
  - Hold the first quarterly recertification at go-live.
  - Add an IAM/Terraform policy linter to CI.
  - Separate admin identities (SEC-017).
- **Owner:** Security — Bhavish Nobeen.
- **Target date:** 2026-11-30 (proposed). The workbook target of 2026-10-30 is not yet due, but the elevation build will not be ready by then.
- **Roadmap phase:** Day 60.

## Risk R-SEC-013
- **Risk:** a compromised ordinary account yields broad data access.
- **Cause:** no formal recertification cycle yet; standing admin rights.
- **Mitigation:** granular roles, owner scoping, MFA for admins, row-level security, and the recertification worksheet.

Likelihood, impact and residual are set in MO-031.
