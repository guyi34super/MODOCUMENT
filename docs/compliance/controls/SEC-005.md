## Requirement
Commit to a measurable SLA backed by SLO monitoring. Acceptance: a
12-month measured availability history exists (start collecting now if it
does not).

## How MoDocument meets it today
- **Commitment written:** 99.5 % monthly availability, maintenance window
  Sundays 07:00–09:00 Mauritius time, measurement method, RPO/RTO, and a
  draft SLA clause — `docs/security/sla-and-status.md` (updated
  2026-10-19). The contract clause itself is `TO CONFIRM (organisational)`.
- **Probe endpoint built and tested:** `GET /api/probe/synthetic` is
  public, rate-limited per IP, and does a real database round trip
  (`src/main/java/com/modocument/api/controller/SyntheticProbeController.java`;
  `src/test/java/com/modocument/api/SyntheticProbeTest.java` — 3 of 3
  passed on 2026-10-19,
  [A8-infra-checks.md](../evidence/A8-infra-checks.md) §2).
- **No external monitor:** no Route 53 health check, CloudWatch Synthetics
  canary or third-party monitor exists in Terraform or the repository
  (TF-96). Without it there is no measurement and no history.
- **Availability design** behind the number: Multi-AZ database, two tasks
  across two AZs, autoscaling, circuit breaker (SEC-004).
- **Alarms:** ALB 5xx alarm as an SLA input
  (`infra/terraform/modules/security-monitoring/main.tf`, TF-95); delivery
  blocked by DEF-2 until the topic gets a customer-managed key.
- **Status page:** none (`TO CONFIRM (organisational)`); in-app
  announcements exist for maintenance notices (SUP-S11).
- **Monthly uptime report:** template written in
  `docs/security/sla-and-status.md`; no report exists (no production).
- **On call:** Bhavish Nobeen and Rushal, weekly rotation (workbook design
  decision); rota and paging tool `TO CONFIRM (organisational)`.

## Questionnaire answer (final)
MO Intelligence commits to 99.5 % monthly availability for MoDocument
(about 3 hours 39 minutes of unplanned downtime a month), excluding an
announced Sunday maintenance window of 07:00–09:00 Mauritius time.
Availability will be measured by an external monitor calling a public
health endpoint that exercises the full path to the database every minute,
and reported monthly. MoDocument is pre-production: the health endpoint is
built and tested, and the monitoring service, the public status page and
the weekly on-call rotation will be in place before go-live, so there is
no measured history yet. Evidence: SEC-005-E01.
