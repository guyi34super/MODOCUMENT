## Requirement
Enforce a modern, configurable password policy per bank. Gate B4 acceptance: `Password123` and
the user's own e-mail local part are rejected, and the sixth wrong attempt locks the account.

## How MoDocument meets it today
- **One policy everywhere a password is set**: password change, admin user creation, admin
  password reset and the bootstrap admins
  (`src/main/java/com/modocument/application/security/PasswordPolicy.java`,
  `src/main/java/com/modocument/application/usecase/AuthUseCase.java`,
  `src/main/java/com/modocument/application/usecase/UserPasswordResetChangeApplier.java`).
- **Length, not composition (NIST SP 800-63B):** minimum 12 characters, configurable per bank
  through the setting `security.password.minLength` (12–128, maker/checker); passphrases up to
  the 200-character request bound are accepted
  (`src/main/java/com/modocument/infrastructure/settings/SettingDefinitions.java`,
  `src/main/java/com/modocument/application/dto/ChangePasswordRequest.java`).
- **Breached/common-password check (code path verified):** `PasswordPolicy.check` rejects any
  password found, case-insensitively, in `src/main/resources/security/common-passwords.txt`
  (11,384 entries, counted 2026-10-19; `password123` is one of them). The check runs before the
  length check, so `Password123` is called a common password.
- **User-derived values:** a password containing the e-mail local part (3+ characters) is
  rejected.
- **Lockout with backoff:** after 5 wrong passwords (bank setting 3–10) the next one locks the
  account for 1 minute, doubling per lockout up to 30 minutes (base 1–60, cap 30–1440); the
  response stays a generic 401 and the event is audited `ACCOUNT_LOCKED`
  (`src/main/java/com/modocument/application/security/LoginLockoutPolicy.java`). Sign-in is also
  rate-limited to 30 attempts per address and 10 per account per 15 minutes.
- **Keycloak path:** realm policy `length(12) and notUsername and notEmail`, brute-force
  protection on (`docker/keycloak/modocument-realm.json`). Entra ID password rules are the
  bank tenant's (`TO CONFIRM (organisational)`).

## Questionnaire answer (final)
Yes. MoDocument enforces a password policy based on NIST SP 800-63B for every password it holds:
at least 12 characters (each bank can raise this up to 128), long passphrases allowed, and
rejection of passwords on a built-in list of common and breached passwords and of passwords that
contain the user's own e-mail name. After five wrong attempts the account locks, for 1 minute at
first and doubling up to 30 minutes; each bank can tune these values within fixed limits. Users
who sign in through Keycloak get the same minimum length plus username and e-mail checks; users
who sign in through Microsoft Entra ID follow their bank's Entra password policy. Evidence:
SEC-021-E01.

## Risk R-SEC-021
Password spraying succeeds against common passwords. Cause in the workbook: length rule only and
breached passwords accepted. Today the breached-list check, lockout with backoff and per-address
and per-account throttles address this cause. Likelihood, impact and residual are set in MO-031.
