# SEC-017 — Privileged access rights restricted, controlled and monitored

## Requirement
Keep the privileged population small, justified and watched. The number of privileged accounts must be countable, and each one must map to a ticket.

## How MoDocument meets it today
**In the application**, privileged access is small, controlled and monitored by design:

- **Small and bounded.**
  - Platform staff hold `PLATFORM_ADMIN` only in the single platform bank (`src/main/java/com/modocument/application/security/RoleAssignmentPolicy.java`; the V210 trigger in `src/main/resources/db/migration/V210__ws21_platform_bank.sql`).
  - Bootstrap creates exactly two platform admins, and the platform can never fall below two active ones (`MIN_ACTIVE_PLATFORM_ADMINS`; `src/main/java/com/modocument/infrastructure/bootstrap/BootstrapPlatformRunner.java`).
  - `PLATFORM_ADMIN` holds no bank data permission (`src/main/java/com/modocument/application/security/RolePermissions.java`).
- **Controlled.**
  - Every grant, role change, disable, password reset and MFA reset needs a second person (`docs/security/maker-checker.md`). So does every platform action except reactivating a bank.
  - Self-approval is refused with 409. The live probe confirmed this for a bank change and for a platform `BANK_CREATE` (`docs/compliance/evidence/A4-rbac-probe.md`, scenarios 1 and 19).
  - A bank admin cannot change their own role (scenario 21) and cannot create a `PLATFORM_ADMIN` (scenario 22).
- **Strong authentication.** MFA is always enforced for `PLATFORM_ADMIN`, and is locked on for `ADMIN` (`src/main/java/com/modocument/application/usecase/AuthUseCase.java`).
- **Monitored.**
  - Every privileged action writes an append-only audit row (`docs/security/audit-events.md`).
  - The `privileged-change` alert fires immediately on role changes, MFA removal, password and MFA resets, user-list export and DSR export or erase.
  - The `platform-change` alert (HIGH) fires on bank onboarding, suspension and break-glass admin creation.
  - The `admin-login` alert fires on ADMIN sign-in (`src/main/resources/application.yml`, `docs/security/alerting-and-export-caps.md`).
- **Countable.** Privileged accounts can be listed per bank with the user list, its CSV export (with last login) and the access-review worksheet. All three were exercised live (probe scenarios 38–39).

**Around the application:**

- The RDS master password is held by AWS Secrets Manager (`manage_master_user_password = true` in `infra/terraform/modules/rds/main.tf`).
- DDL statements and connections are logged by the RDS parameter group (same file).
- CloudTrail is defined in `infra/terraform/modules/security-monitoring/main.tf`.
- None of this Terraform is applied yet, so live evidence is `NOT IN REPO (live cloud)`.

The workbook's owner statements cannot be proved from the repository: two administrators (Bhavish Nobeen and Rushal) across AWS, Azure and MoDocument; root sealed in a password manager with an alert on use. `TO CONFIRM (organisational)`

**Policy document:** `docs/compliance/policies/privileged-access-and-break-glass.md`. It holds the privileged-account inventory, the break-glass procedure, the monthly review procedure and a first record. The record is a dry run on the E2E stack, not a production review.

## Questionnaire answer (final)
Privileged access is limited to a small, named group and every grant is dual-controlled:

- In MoDocument, MO Intelligence's platform administrators sit in a dedicated platform tenant, with no access to client data.
- There are always exactly two or more of them, and each one's changes must be approved by the other.
- Each bank's administrators manage only their own bank, and every user or role change they make needs a second person's approval.
- Self-approval is blocked by the system.
- Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all administrators.
- Every privileged action is audited and raises an immediate alert.

The cloud administrators are the two named founders, with root credentials sealed and alerted on use (to be confirmed with the infrastructure evidence). Separate administrator identities, the written grant record and the monthly privileged-activity review are being put in place. The proposed completion date is 30 November 2026. Evidence SEC-017-E01.

## Test SEC-017-T01
- **Procedure:**
  1. Identify the privileged population: P1–P6 in the policy.
  2. Enumerate the in-product population on the live stack.
  3. Try self-approval and privilege escalation.
  4. Check that each account maps to a ticket.
- **Expected result:** the privileged account count is countable, and every account maps to a ticket.
- **Actual result:**
  - The in-product population could be enumerated (user list, CSV export and access-review worksheet, 7 accounts in the test bank).
  - Self-approval was refused for a bank change and a platform change (409).
  - A bank admin could neither change their own role (409) nor create a platform admin (400).
  - No ticket mapping exists: there is no production population, and the grant record for the two existing cloud administrators is not yet written.
- **Result:** Partial · tester: Agent A4 (automated re-performance); human sign-off required · date: 2026-10-19

## Gap and remediation
- **Gap:**
  - Everyday accounts double as admin accounts (workbook, organisational).
  - No written grant record for the two existing administrators.
  - No monthly review held yet.
  - No role elevation (AWS IAM Identity Center / Azure PIM) in the Terraform.
  - `PLATFORM_ADMIN` sign-ins are not covered by the `admin-login` alert (MO-019 X-05).
  - `BANK_ADMIN_ADD` lets the platform maker choose the new ADMIN's temporary password (MO-019 X-04).
- **Gap type:** Org.
- **Action:**
  - Bhavish Nobeen separates admin identities through role elevation.
  - Write the grant record for the existing grants, approved by Reezvi Pydiah.
  - Hold the first monthly review with the policy's template.
  - Extend the `admin-login` alert to `PLATFORM_ADMIN`.
- **Owner:** Security — Bhavish Nobeen.
- **Target date:** 2026-10-30 (workbook) for the grant record and identity separation. 2026-11-30 (proposed) for the alert change and the first monthly review.
- **Roadmap phase:** Day 30.

## Risk R-SEC-017
- **Risk:** unknown or shared admin accounts operate without being watched.
- **Cause:** a small founding team, whose admin rights were granted to their daily identities from the start.
- **Mitigation:**
  - In the product: a two-admin minimum, maker/checker, mandatory MFA, immediate alerts and an append-only audit trail.
  - Outside the product: the policy above.

Likelihood, impact and residual are set in MO-031.
