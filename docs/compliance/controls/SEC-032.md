## Requirement
Detect and notify on security-relevant events within minutes, with
configurable thresholds. Acceptance: all tripped test rules notify in less
than 5 minutes.

## How MoDocument meets it today
- **Alert-rule catalogue with thresholds:** 26 rules over the append-only
  audit trail, configuration not code (`modocument.alerts.rules` in
  `src/main/resources/application.yml`), validated fail-fast at startup
  (`src/main/java/com/modocument/infrastructure/alerting/AlertRuleCatalogue.java`)
  and pinned by `AlertRuleCatalogueTest`. Full table with triggers,
  thresholds, windows, severities and env overrides:
  `docs/security/alerting-and-export-caps.md` (updated 2026-10-19 with the
  three rules it was missing).
- **Covers the workbook's catalogue:** failed logins per user and per IP
  (≥ 5 in 5 min), lockout, refresh-token replay, privileged-role grants and
  MFA removal (`privileged-change`), self-approval attempts
  (`sod-attempt-blocked`), SoD violations from the nightly scan, bulk exports
  (`export-blocked`, `export-over-cap`, `export-burst`), admin bursts,
  authorization-denied and access-denied bursts, API-client auth-failure
  spikes, AI refusals, platform changes. Not covered: login from a new
  country / impossible travel, off-hours privileged activity as its own rule,
  new egress destination, canary-row access, AWS root-account use.
- **Configurable thresholds:** each windowed rule's threshold and window is
  also a platform setting (`alerts.<rule>.threshold` / `.window`, maker/checker
  approved), applied without a restart (`docs/security/alerting-and-export-caps.md`
  "Platform tuning"; `SettingsConsumersOverrideTest`).
- **Engine and evidence:** immediate rules fire after commit of the audit row;
  windowed rules are evaluated by a sweep every 60 s; each alert is stored in
  `AlertLog` with created/delivered times and channel acceptance is audited
  `ALERT_SENT` (`src/main/java/com/modocument/infrastructure/alerting/AlertService.java`).
  Admins see them under Administration → Security alerts
  (`frontend/src/pages/AdminAlertsPage.jsx`).
- **Channels:** log (always), webhook (the phone path) and e-mail
  (`src/main/java/com/modocument/infrastructure/alerting/WebhookAlertChannel.java`,
  `src/main/java/com/modocument/infrastructure/alerting/EmailAlertChannel.java`).
  **No receiver is configured**: the ECS task definition passes no
  `MODOCUMENT_ALERTS_*` variables (`infra/terraform/modules/ecs-app/main.tf`),
  so in AWS only the log channel would be active. The owner statement
  "alerts go to e-mail only" cannot be verified in the repository.
- **Escalation policy:** `docs/compliance/policies/alert-escalation-policy.md`
  (on-call rotation Bhavish Nobeen / Rushal, response targets per severity,
  escalation to the incident owner, the DPO and Reezvi Pydiah).

## Questionnaire answer (final)
Security alerts are raised in real time from the audit trail by 26
configurable rules, including repeated failed logins per account and per
address, account lockouts, refresh-token replay, privileged role and MFA
changes, self-approval attempts, separation-of-duties violations, blocked and
large exports, bursts of denied access, API-client authentication failures
and platform changes. Thresholds and windows are configuration and can be
tuned by administrators under four-eyes approval. Single-event rules fire
immediately and threshold rules are evaluated every minute; in our automated
test run of 19 October 2026, every tripped immediate rule was raised within
0.2 seconds and delivered to its channels within 0.4 seconds. Alerts are
recorded with their delivery evidence and sent to the log, a webhook and
e-mail. Routing of high-severity alerts to the on-call phone, with a written
on-call and escalation policy, is being completed by 30 October 2026.
Evidence SEC-032-E01.

## Test SEC-032-T01
- **Procedure:** run the alerting tests (`AlertingGateD1Test`,
  `ExportCapGateD2Test`, `AccessDeniedBurstAlertTest`,
  `PlatformChangeAlertTest`, `AlertServiceTest`, `AlertRuleCatalogueTest`,
  `AgentOfflineSweepTest`); measure from the run log the time from each
  triggering audit row to the alert and to channel acceptance.
- **Expected result:** all tripped test rules notify in less than 5 minutes.
- **Actual result:** all tests pass. 11 immediate alerts (rules
  `export-blocked`, `export-over-cap`, `account-locked`, `admin-login`,
  `platform-change`, `sod-attempt-blocked`) were raised within 108 ms and
  accepted by the log and test-webhook channels within 346 ms. 12 windowed
  alerts (`login-failures-per-user`, `login-failures-per-ip`,
  `access-denied-burst`) were raised when the tests ran the sweep;
  `AlertingGateD1Test` asserts creation within 5 minutes; production worst
  case is one 60 s sweep plus delivery. Rules tripped end to end: 10 of 26.
  No person's phone or mailbox was in the loop.
- **Result:** Partial · tester: Agent A5 (automated re-performance; human
  sign-off required) · date: 2026-10-19.

## Risk R-SEC-032
Attacks proceed for days; the first notice comes from the bank or the
attacker. Cause: alerting set up for daytime monitoring only, no receiver
connected. Mitigation: phone path and escalation policy; trip tests per rule.
Likelihood, impact and residual are set in MO-031.
