## Questionnaire answer (final)
We maintain a dated security roadmap from October 2026 to Q1 2027, with a
longer standing cadence of quarterly access reviews, semi-annual restore
drills and an annual penetration test and crypto review. Each action has an
owner, a target date, a status, the artifact that proves progress and the
budget line it needs. Current-quarter items include remediation of
dependency vulnerabilities, routing security alerts to the on-call phone,
enforcing the network egress allow-list, session recording and database
audit logging, a reference SIEM test, the security contact and disclosure
policy, and the decision on an independent penetration test (budget decision
due 31 October 2026, test and retest in Q1 2027). The roadmap is reviewed
monthly by the directors. Evidence SEC-041-E01.
